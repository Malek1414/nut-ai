## Accounts and filming notes

- D1 creates account and membership tables on first use. Passkeys are bound to the configured `AUTH_ORIGIN`; use `http://localhost:5199` (not the numeric IP) in development. Production is bound to the HTTPS Worker origin. Password-based accounts use salted PBKDF2 in the Worker with throttling. There is no email recovery service. Session cookies are HTTP-only and the Worker stores only their hashes.
- Signing in imports the device’s community posts, saved picks, joined communities and goal preferences. The log and meal builder remain on the device; assistant chat stays in memory until reload or logout. Use the browser’s speech and read-aloud support when available; typing is always available.
- **Before filming:** test the mic, camera, and web passkey prompt on the physical phone over HTTPS. If launched from an embedded browser, open in Safari or Chrome for permissions. Test A and B with attached menu data for the intended area. Berlin examples and mocked camera results in these screenshots are not evidence of current menus. Confirm ingredients with restaurant staff.
- No Google services, Workers AI, paid speech/auth services, or new model credentials were added. The assistant uses the app’s existing Anthropic configuration, with per-account and shared beta request limits. Nut AI attribution and the AGPL license remain intact.
